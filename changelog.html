What's new: 漢字 strokes

The most recent changes are listed first. Not every small fix makes the list, just the bits worth knowing about.

18 October 2026
  • New "How this app works" and "What's new" pages, linked from the top of Settings. The ⚙ turns green when there's something new here.
  • Back up my progress: Settings can now save your scores to a file and restore them, like the other two apps.
  • The ⚙ and sound buttons have moved up beside the app's name, to match the Japanese drill and Kanji cards.
  • The search box has a × to clear it, and a round search button beside it.
17 October 2026
  • Contracted pairs: きゃ to ぴょ and キャ to ピョ, drawn as a pair so you can see how small the second character is.
  • Dakuten and handakuten (が to ぽ, ヴ) and the small kana (ゃ ゅ ょ っ…, ー) are now on the hiragana and katakana charts.
  • Drawing a dakuten's two marks the wrong way round is now spotted as wrong order.
16 October 2026
  • Hold any tile to peek at it: its stroke order, meaning and readings, read out loud. The new speaker button turns sound off.
  • Proper back navigation: a "← …" link at the top left of inner pages, and your phone's back gesture now goes one step back.
  • Settings shows how to install the app if it isn't installed yet, and has a QR code for sharing it, with links to the Japanese drill and Kanji cards.
  • The app always keeps a copy for when you have no signal.
Before that
  • What's here: hiragana, katakana and 500 kanji to practise in three steps (Watch, Trace, Recall), a drawing grade after each character, scores and colours on every tile, and Look up by drawing for all the jōyō kanji.
